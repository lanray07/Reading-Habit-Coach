## Short description

Track your books, reading time and pages. Keep a private reading journal and build consistency with flexible weekly goals. No account needed.

## Long description

Read more consistently. Remember what matters.

Reading Habit Coach gives your reading life a calm, private home. Organise physical books, audiobooks, library ebooks and compatible personal documents in one library. Keep track of what you want to read, what you are reading and the books you have finished.

Make room for a few good pages. Start, pause and finish a reading session, or quickly log reading you have already done. Record minutes and page progress without converting audiobook listening into invented page counts.

Build a habit at your own pace. Choose daily reading minutes and weekly reading days. Your confirmed weekly consistency is based on completed weeks, so there is room for days off. Your lifetime reading records stay with you when a streak ends.

Keep the ideas worth remembering. Save quotes, questions, reflections and summaries in a searchable journal. Link entries to books, add tags and favourites, and export your notes.

Read compatible personal documents. The EPUB reader includes adjustable type, spacing, themes, chapters, saved positions and bookmarks. The PDF reader provides page navigation, zoom and bookmarks. This build does not include PDF text search, OCR or text highlighting, protected Kindle support or a Kindle library integration.

Use text commands on every supported device. Where compatible system services are installed, speak supported localized example commands and listen to notes or supported EPUB text. Recognition may need an internet connection. Complete touch controls remain available.

Your library, sessions and journal are stored locally. No account is required. Optional online metadata search sends only your query to Open Library. Export JSON records, session CSV or journal notes through the document picker. Document files are not silently included in backups.

Early-access build: localized interface with 21 locale configurations. Native-speaker review remains pending. Premium checkout and cloud backup are not yet available. All current features are open to explore with no ads or configured in-app charges. Requires Android API 26 or later; device-specific voice capabilities vary.

## Metadata and image specification check

Checked against [Amazon's Appstore Details documentation](https://developer.amazon.com/docs/app-submission/appstore-details.html) on 20 October 2026:

- Short description: 2,000-byte limit; keep it brief and put the value first.
- Long description: at most 4,000 characters, plain text. Three to five feature bullets.
- Tablet icons: 114×114 and 512×512 PNG; render from vector source instead of enlarging a small PNG.
- Screenshots: 3–10 images; allowed sizes include 800×480, 1024×600, 1280×720, 1280×800, 1920×1080, 1920×1200 and 2560×1600, portrait or landscape, PNG/JPEG.
- Promotional art: 1024×500 PNG/JPEG; represent the brand, keep text 50 px from edges, and omit price, ratings, buttons and screenshots.

Recheck the Console before submission. This document does not confirm account-level title acceptance, availability, device eligibility or store approval. Current screenshots must reflect the implemented app; do not advertise future features or substitute generated UI for actual screen captures.
